# Set up Business Guru

Business Guru works best inside a Project so the instructions and the user’s saved Business Guru Profile can be reused across chats. Product labels and availability may vary by plan or workspace.

Keep the Project focused. Business Guru should use only the files, profile, and context intentionally added to that Project. Do not connect unrelated workspaces or memories unless you want them included.

## ChatGPT

1. Download `SKILL.md` and keep this setup guide nearby.
2. Create a new ChatGPT Project named **Business Guru**.
3. Add `SKILL.md` to the Project’s files or sources.
4. In Project settings, add this instruction:

   `Use the Business Guru instructions in SKILL.md for business decisions, workflows, delegation, strategy, and project breakdown. When I say “Open Business Guru,” begin its guided setup unless I already gave you a specific problem.`

5. Start a new chat in the Project and say: `Open Business Guru.`
6. Complete the guided questions. Ask Business Guru to create your **Business Guru Profile**.
7. Save that profile to the same Project as a source. Review it before adding confidential information.

For a one-time session, attach `SKILL.md` directly to a normal chat and use the same opening message. Continuity across later chats is not guaranteed unless the relevant context is saved in a place the host can reuse.

Official reference: [Projects in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt)

## Claude

1. Download `SKILL.md`.
2. Create a Claude Project named **Business Guru**. Projects are subject to Anthropic’s current plan availability.
3. Add `SKILL.md` to the Project knowledge base.
4. Set the Project instructions to:

   `Use the Business Guru instructions in SKILL.md for business decisions, workflows, delegation, strategy, and project breakdown. When I say “Open Business Guru,” begin its guided setup unless I already gave you a specific problem.`

5. Start a normal **Chat** inside the Project, not Cowork or Code, and say: `Open Business Guru.`
6. Complete the guided questions and ask for your **Business Guru Profile**.
7. Add the final profile to Project knowledge if you want it available in future Project chats.

Anthropic notes that context is not automatically shared across Project chats unless it has been added to Project knowledge.

Official reference: [Create and manage Claude Projects](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/9519177-how-can-i-create-and-manage-projects)

## First useful prompts

- `Open Business Guru and help me untangle a decision.`
- `Map this workflow from the outcome backward.`
- `Show me what requires my judgment, what someone else can own, and what belongs in a system.`
- `Turn this overwhelming project into the smallest useful next steps.`
- `Pressure-test this strategy and tell me what evidence would change your recommendation.`

## Keep it current

At the end of a useful session, ask:

`What, if anything, should be added to my Business Guru Profile?`

Review the proposed update before saving it. The skill should never silently turn a momentary preference or a difficult week into a permanent rule about how the user works.
