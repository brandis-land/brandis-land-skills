# Business Guru

Business Guru is a guided business thinking partner for founders, leaders, and small-business owners. It helps turn complex work into a clear decision, workable workflow, delegation plan, or smallest useful next step.

Its central rule is simple: lead the business and work within the system without becoming the system.

## What it helps with

- business decisions and strategic tradeoffs;
- workflow mapping and repair;
- delegation and ownership;
- prioritization and capacity;
- project breakdown; and
- externalizing work that currently depends on memory or constant founder involvement.

Business Guru is designed to be neurodivergent-affirming without assuming that every user is neurodivergent or turning a working style into a diagnosis.

## Install

Download this folder, then follow the [ChatGPT or Claude setup guide](references/setup-guide.md).

The shortest start is:

1. Add `SKILL.md` to a ChatGPT or Claude Project.
2. Add the Project instruction shown in the setup guide.
3. Start a chat with: `Open Business Guru.`

## What is included

- `SKILL.md`: the core behavior and guided workflow;
- `references/setup-guide.md`: beginner-friendly ChatGPT and Claude setup; and
- `references/working-profile.md`: a reusable business context template.
